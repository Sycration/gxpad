sgweb

Construct. Constrain. Derive.

Draw points, lines, circles and conics, tie them together with constraints and dimensions, then select a measurement: sgweb finds a closed-form formula for it.

    Using sgweb

    How it works

    Pick a tool and tap on the canvas: the hint at the top says what each step wants. Taps snap to points and curves, so new points can sit on what is already there.

    Select items to see what you can do with them: constraints such as parallel or tangent, and measurements. A driving dimension sets a value; a measured one only reads it. Select a dimension or variable to get a formula for it in the Result tab.

    On a touch screen, tapping adds to the selection; tap empty space to clear it. Drag with one finger to move things or pan, pinch with two to zoom. With a drawing tool, the preview follows your finger and the point goes where you lift it.
